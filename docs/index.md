# What is Otari?

Otari is an open-source AI gateway by [Mozilla AI](https://www.mozilla.ai/) that provides a unified control plane for your AI infrastructure. 

You can run it yourself for free, or use the hosted version at [otari.ai](https://otari.ai/) ([see pricing](https://otari.ai/#pricing)).

## What you can do

* [**Organize**](organize.md) your organization into workspaces, members, and providers.
* [**Build**](build.md) on models from any provider, with tools and your own agents.
* [**Control**](control.md) who can call what, how much they spend, and what happens when a provider fails.
* [**Observe**](observe.md) what your apps and agents did, and what it cost.
