# Data Collection

Otari collects and processes data necessary to operate, secure, and improve its services. This includes account information, usage data, and metadata associated with API requests.

## **Account Information**

We collect information such as your name, email address, and account details to manage your account, provide support, and communicate service-related updates.

## **Location & Interaction Data**

We collect IP addresses, approximate location information, website and service usage data, browser and device details, and referral information. We may also use cookies and third-party analytics tools to monitor interactions, improve our services, and support legal and regulatory compliance.

## **Usage Data & Metadata**

Otari tracks API request metadata, including token consumption, model and provider usage, latency, and costs. This information supports usage analytics, monitoring, billing, and troubleshooting.

## **AI Requests**

Prompts and responses are processed to route requests to the selected AI providers. If agent session content is enabled, Otari may also store prompts, tool-related information, and data evaluated by guardrail checks, and, where your workspace has turned it on, model responses. Otari also stores a model response when you ask it to: a response to a request sent with an idempotency key is kept for a limited period so the request can be safely retried, and a Playground conversation you choose to save keeps its replies until you delete it. Data handling and retention policies may vary depending on the AI provider and your workspace configuration.

For more information about how we collect, process, store, and protect your data, please refer to our [Privacy Policy](https://otari.ai/privacy).
