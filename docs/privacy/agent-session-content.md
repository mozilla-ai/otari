# Agent Session Content

Otari may collect and store agent session content to support session monitoring, troubleshooting, and visibility into agent activity. This collection is optional and depends on your workspace administrator's settings and your agent's configuration. When enabled, session content may include prompts, tool commands, file paths, and information evaluated by guardrails. Session content is encrypted, subject to access controls, and retained for a limited period, as described below.

If an administrator of your workspace turns on session content, and your agent is configured to send it, Mozilla.ai will store the prompt that starts each turn of an agent session, the commands and file paths the agent's tools use, and the information our guardrail checks evaluated. We do not store tool output or model responses. We collect this content only for current members of the workspace; content already stored stays until it is deleted as described below, including after you leave.

Session content is encrypted using keys held in AWS Key Management Service in the region your workspace is hosted in, with a separate key for each session.

You can choose to share session content with other members of your workspace or organization, for up to 90 days. Session content sharing ends when you end it or when you leave the workspace or the organization and does not resume if you return. Other members of your workspace, your organization's administrators and Mozilla.ai personnel can see that a session took place and what it cost, but the Service does not show them its content unless you have shared that session or the owner or an administrator for your organization has requested access to your session content (as explained below). When you share a session, the Service records when another user reads the shared session content and you can view this data in the Service.

**Administrative access to session content**. Your organization's owners or administrators (“owners/admins”) can open the content of any of the organization's sessions regardless of whether you have shared the content, and Mozilla.ai can do so as well where required to comply with the law or legal process or where necessary to protect the rights or safety of others. Mozilla.ai records a reason for each such access. When your organization's owners/admins access content, your session content is visible to other owners/admins for your organization, but not to Mozilla.ai; access to session content by Mozilla.ai is visible to Mozilla.ai's operators but the content is not shown to your organization (unless accessed by an owner/admin, separately). Users are not notified when an owner/admin or Mozilla.ai has accessed unshared content.  Records of such access are retained for one year (or longer where required by law or necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Mozilla.ai or others), including after your account or your organization is deleted.  

Session content is deleted together with the session 30 days after its last activity and 90 days after it started at the latest, when a workspace administrator deletes it, or when your user is deleted. Copies may persist in our encrypted backups until those backups expire.

Agent session content (and associated provider credentials) are encrypted at rest. The keys are managed in AWS Key Management Service and can be used only by the running Service, and AWS records each time the Service obtains one. Access to session content is controlled by the Service and recorded; our operational procedures restrict and record direct access to the production environment.

For more information about agent session content, please refer to our [Privacy Policy](https://otari.ai/privacy).
